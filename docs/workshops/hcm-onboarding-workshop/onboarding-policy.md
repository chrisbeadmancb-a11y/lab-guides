# Remote new starter onboarding policy

Fictional training policy · Version 1.0

Use this reference with [the policy advisor exercise](?lab=policy-advisor) or [the Journeys checklist exercise](?lab=journeys-ai-assist).

## 1. Scope

This policy covers employees starting a remote role in the workshop scenario.

## 2. Equipment

The hiring manager requests the laptop through IT at least five business days before the employee’s start date. The IT service desk ships the equipment and records delivery progress on that request.

## 3. Account access

Before the employee’s first day, the IT service desk sends an invitation to set up the work account. If the invitation is missing by noon in the new starter’s local time on the last business day before their start date, the hiring manager follows up on the original IT ticket.

## 4. First week

The employee uses the invitation to activate the work account, attends the team orientation and checks the work contact details in their HR profile. If the invitation or equipment is missing when the employee starts, they tell the hiring manager. The manager follows up on the original IT ticket.
