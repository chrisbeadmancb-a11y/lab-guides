# After the Chicago sales trip

## About this lab

Allow **20 minutes** for the three exercises.

Casey Brown and his sales team are back from Chicago. Their fitness-company customer has given them a verbal commitment on a loan, but Casey damaged his laptop on the trip. Start in Oracle Fusion with Casey’s replacement request and quarterly access review. Then switch to Oracle EPM as Kerry Lane to investigate an unexpected increase in smartphone revenue.

### Exercises

1. [Requisition a new laptop](?lab=requisition-new-laptop) — find the policy for a nonstandard laptop, check a pending request, and open the details of an order on hold.
2. [Risk management and separation of duties](?lab=separation-of-duties) — inspect a procurement role, export its security briefing, and practise documenting a decision.
3. [Anomaly insights](?lab=anomaly-insights) — compare regional sales, explain the East region’s revenue spike, and ask a colleague to review it.
