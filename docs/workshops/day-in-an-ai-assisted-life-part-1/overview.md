# Manage a sales opportunity with AI

## About this lab

A sales VP and three sales associates have just met with a fitness company in Chicago. The customer wants to expand to a new location and has given the team a verbal commitment on a loan. Back at your desk as Casey Brown, you have two people to follow up with. Oswald needs a meeting to review the loan terms, and Natalie needs feedback on her work with the customer.

This lab takes about **10 minutes**. You’ll use the pre-built AI features in Oracle Fusion Customer Experience to manage the sales opportunity, then use AI Assist in Feedback Center.

### Objectives

1. [Sales follow-ups](?lab=sales-follow-ups) — use Deal Advisor to identify risks and next steps, then generate, review, and send a follow-up email to Oswald.
2. [Generate feedback](?lab=generate-feedback) — turn notes from the meeting into feedback that recognises Natalie’s product knowledge and gives her a concrete next step.
