## Before you begin

Open a blank document in a text editor, such as Notepad on Windows or TextEdit on a Mac. In TextEdit, choose **Format → Make Plain Text** if you are working in rich text.

## 1. Copy the template

Use **Copy** on the block below and paste it into your blank document. You can also select the lines and use your device’s usual copy command.

```text
Lab notes

What I tried

What I observed

Next step
```

## 2. Record your first result

Under **What I tried**, write `Copied the note template into my editor.` Under **What I observed**, record whether the three headings and their blank lines came across. Under **Next step**, add one thing you want to learn in the workshop.

## 3. Save and check

Save the file as `lab-notes.txt`. Close it, reopen it, and look for the goal you wrote under **Next step**. Once you can see it, your log is ready for the next lab.
